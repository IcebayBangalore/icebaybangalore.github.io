Our story

Born in Kollam. Now loved across India.

IceBay was founded in 2022 in Kollam, Kerala, by a group of passionate dreamers with one mission: spread happiness, one ice pop at a time.

Every stick is made with fresh fruit and natural ingredients, in flavours people don't expect from an ice pop. Alongside the sticks you'll find scoops, sundaes, waffles, shakes and falooda.

Today there are 180+ IceBay outlets across India, and four in Bangalore, from the tech parks of Electronic City to the food courts of Whitefield.
